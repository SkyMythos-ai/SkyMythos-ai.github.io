Enterprise Knowledge supports collaborative team maintenance, controlled external document imports, and ongoing quality analytics so shared team documentation stays accurate and trustworthy.

## Workspace Collaboration & Ownership [#workspace-collaboration-and-roles]

Inside a `Workspace Knowledge Base`, members collaborate according to their workspace roles:

- **Drafting & Conflict Safety**: Authorized maintainers edit drafts with full revision tracking. If a concurrent save or publish conflict occurs, reload the latest revision and merge changes carefully instead of overwriting teammates' work.
- **External vs. Native Editing Rights**: Externally synced documents preserve their source provenance and may remain read-only inside SkyMythos so teams update truth in the upstream origin rather than creating diverging local forks.

## Explicit Lark / Feishu Import & Sync Jobs [#lark-doc-sync-and-jobs]

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Verify App Scopes & Grants

    Confirm an Org Admin enabled document grants (`docs:document.content:read` and `offline_access`) under [Lark, Feishu & Slack Setup](/docs/im-integration).
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Import by Explicit Docx URL

    Workspace Admins provide a specific Lark/Feishu Docx URL to pull Markdown into a target Knowledge Base. **SkyMythos never bulk-crawls entire external workspaces or claims to mirror external ACL trees automatically.**
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Monitor Sync Jobs

    Open `Knowledge Sync` (`knowledge/sync`) in the context sidebar to track import jobs, destination bases, and failure reasons, retrying individual jobs once URL or token issues are fixed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

- **Spot Stale or Unsourced Entries**: Audit outdated documents, entries missing maintainers, or guidelines superseded by newer decisions; update drafts, publish new revisions, and re-run key retrieval checks.
- **Archive Instead of Destructive Deletion**: When retiring obsolete documents referenced by past tasks, check downstream impact and archive cleanly rather than deleting bases teams still rely on.
- **Keep Transcripts Out of Knowledge**: Never dump raw chat logs directly into authoritative Knowledge Bases—route distilled insights through [Memory](/docs/memory) or [AI Employee](/docs/employees) review flows first.
