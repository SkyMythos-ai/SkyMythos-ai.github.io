Enterprise Knowledge connects team documentation, immutable release revisions, and authorized retrieval pipelines. SkyMythos keeps **saving a draft, publishing a release, completing indexing**, and **being adopted by a task** strictly separate so every fact entering an AI workflow remains verifiable.

## Knowledge Scopes & Access Matrix [#knowledge-scopes-and-permissions]

Open **Knowledge** (`knowledge`) from the primary rail to view collections accessible in your active workspace. Knowledge Bases fall into four ownership and visibility tiers:

| Scope Tier | Ownership | Read & Management Permissions | Typical Use Case |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Personal Knowledge Base | Individual User | Visible and editable only by the owner unless explicitly shared | Personal research notes and private reference drafts |
| Workspace Knowledge Base | Active Workspace | Maintained and queried collaboratively according to workspace roles | Team standards, architectural decisions, feedback archives |
| Shared Knowledge Base | Original User or Workspace | Grants authorized read/query access without transferring ownership | Cross-team read-only reference to upstream department policies |
| Public Knowledge Release | Original Maintainer (Snapshot) | Discoverable and queryable by any signed-in member in the Org | Company-wide security baselines, onboarding guides, tech specs |

<Callout type="info" title="Supported Media & External Sync Scope">
  The Knowledge ingestion pipeline accepts **text/Markdown, PDF, and images**. For Lark/Feishu sync, Workspace Admins import specific documents by explicit Docx URL—the platform does not bulk-crawl entire external spaces or claim to mirror external ACLs automatically.
</Callout>

## Authoring, Publishing & Indexing Workflow [#create-publish-and-index]

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Create or Upload Documents

    Inside a Knowledge Base where you hold management rights, create a new document inline or upload text, PDF, or image files. Include applicability conditions, factual sources, maintainers, and re-verification criteria inside the body.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Save Draft

    Click **Save Draft** and confirm the success notice. Unsaved edits in the browser editor are not persisted and do not enter any release.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Publish Per-Document Release

    Open the document detail view, click **Publish New Version**, and confirm. Publishing snapshots the **saved draft** into an immutable version per document—there is no bulk action that forces every draft across a Knowledge Base into a single release.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Verify Asynchronous Indexing Status

    After publication, the dedicated Embedding Gateway and LightRAG parse and vectorize the content. Monitor the status column as it progresses from `Unpublished` → `Processing` → `Searchable`. If `Failed` appears, inspect the error details and fix underlying gateway or document issues before retrying; `Unknown` indicates missing confirmation rather than success.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Retrieval Testing & Source Provenance [#retrieval-testing-and-provenance]

Before relying on a Knowledge Base in production tasks, verify recall using the built-in **Retrieval Test**:

1. Open the **Retrieval Test** view inside the target Knowledge Base, enter a natural-language question or keywords (e.g., "Who approves customer feedback priorities?"), and click **Test Retrieval**.
2. Search operates strictly on **published and indexed** revisions—unpublished drafts are excluded.
3. Inspect each hit's document title, matched passage, release version, and score. Click the title to jump straight to that exact source revision and verify the surrounding context.

<Callout type="warn" title="Similarity Scores Do Not Prove Truth">
  A high retrieval score only indicates lexical or semantic proximity—always inspect the original document revision. Externally synced documents with read-only restrictions should be updated in the upstream system rather than duplicated locally.
</Callout>

## Sync Tasks, Quality Analytics & Maintenance [#sync-quality-and-lifecycle]

- **Sync & Quality Views**: Use `Knowledge Sync` (`knowledge/sync`) and `Knowledge Quality` (`knowledge/quality`) in the context sidebar to track external import jobs and spot stale or low-quality entries.
- **Updating & Archiving**: To update knowledge, edit the draft, publish a new release, and re-test key queries. Historical citations preserve their original revision identity. Archive retired documents carefully after checking downstream references instead of deleting collections still used by teams.
- **Knowledge vs. Memory Boundary**: Asynchronously distilled `Memory Candidates` serve personal preferences and project continuity after review—never dump raw chat logs wholesale into authoritative Knowledge Bases.

If retrieval returns nothing or indexing repeatedly fails, check the Embedding Gateway setup in [Model Gateways & Credentials](/docs/models-tools) or consult [Troubleshooting & Diagnostics](/docs/troubleshooting).
