title: Right Sidebar & Platform Assistant

From the right sidebar of the Web Console, you can open or pin the **Platform Assistant** at any time. Unlike workspace AI employees or task execution agents, the Platform Assistant serves as a **deployment-wide guide and governed authoring helper** for all authorized users.

## Role & Safety Boundaries [#role-and-boundaries]

- **Page-Aware Guidance**: Regardless of whether you are inspecting Knowledge, Tasks, or Platform Settings, the assistant reads active workspace and page context to explain controls and surface diagnostic hints.
- **Never Bypasses Governed Surfaces**: The Platform Assistant does not replace your execution agents or silently mutate production systems; any real state change routes through explicit confirmation gates.

## Three Core Use Cases [#three-core-use-cases]

<Tabs items={["Product Q&A & Status Checks", "Flow & WorkNode Authoring Aid", "Structured ActionProposals"]}>
  <Tab value="Product Q&A & Status Checks">
    Ask the assistant what a configuration setting does or why a task is paused. It explains domain semantics and points to exact logs without causing background write side effects.
  </Tab>
  <Tab value="Flow & WorkNode Authoring Aid">
    Opening the assistant inside a Flow Template or WorkNode draft launches a **Design Session** pinned to that draft revision and your permissions. Suggested edits arrive as reviewable, reversible **AI Change Sets** that update the draft only after explicit acceptance and Control Plane concurrency (CAS) validation.
  </Tab>
  <Tab value="Structured ActionProposals">
    When suggesting a governed platform action, the assistant renders an `ActionProposal` card detailing target entities and parameters. Nothing runs until you explicitly approve it for the backend `ActionExecutor`.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Panel Controls & Session Scope [#panel-controls]

- **Toggle, Pin & Resize**: Open or collapse the right sidebar from the top utility bar, pin it alongside wide views, and drag the panel resizer to adjust width.
- **Clean Context Separation**: Platform Assistant conversations never bind to your Personal Dev Machine processes or pollute Project directories and AI employee memory.
