SkyMythos divides governance into **Workspace Team Settings** and **Organization Platform Settings**, giving business and engineering teams autonomy inside their workspaces while keeping infrastructure, security policies, and external connectors centrally controlled.

## Dual-Layer Role Matrix [#dual-layer-role-matrix]

| Layer | Role | Core Administrative Scope | Explicit Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Organization | `Admin` | User lifecycle, workspace provisioning, sandbox clusters/images, AI Gateways, enterprise IM & connectors | Platform admin status does not automatically grant read access to private workspace sessions or projects |
| Organization | `Operations` | Full infrastructure ops parity with `Admin` (gateways, clusters, images, runtime monitoring) | Does not manage user account provisioning or workspace membership assignments |
| Organization | `Member` | Participates in authorized workspaces; manages personal credentials and Personal Dev Machine | Automatically joins the system-wide `Public Workspace` |
| Workspace | `Workspace Admin` | Manages workspace members, model quotas, shared credentials, policies, and explicit doc imports | Scoped strictly to the assigned workspace |
| Workspace | `Member` / `Viewer` | `Member` builds and runs projects, tasks, knowledge, and assets; `Viewer` holds read access | Bound by individual resource grants and private credential isolation |

<Callout type="info" title="Account Disable vs. Permanent Deletion">
  All directory members represent real human accounts (legacy machine/service accounts are excluded). **Disabling** preserves memberships and can be reversed; **deleting** revokes all login and membership grants while keeping an immutable tombstone with timestamp and operator. Re-registering with a freed email creates a fresh UUID without transferring historical permissions or private data.
</Callout>

## Team Settings: Workspace Governance [#workspace-team-settings]

Workspace Admins open **Team Settings** (`settings?section=team-overview`) from the primary rail to manage day-to-day collaboration:

- **Member Management**: Assign `Admin`, `Member`, or `Viewer` roles inside the workspace. Removing a member from a workspace only revokes that workspace's membership without affecting their organization account.
- **Usage & Cost Attribution**: Inspect per-member activity, token volume, and known model spend (`team-members` / `team-cost`).
- **Model Quotas & Policies**: Control workspace-approved models, quotas (`workspace-settings?section=models`), shared workspace credentials, and security policies.

## Platform Settings: Infrastructure & Enterprise Integrations [#organization-platform-settings]

Org Admins and Operations engineers manage deployment-wide services under **Platform Settings** (`settings?section=platform-home`):

<Tabs items={["Sandbox Runtimes & Clusters", "Models & Memory Engine", "Enterprise IM & Connectors"]}>
  <Tab value="Sandbox Runtimes & Clusters">
    Under **Runtime Environments**, operators configure:
    - **Kubernetes & E2B Sandbox Pools**: Manage built-in Kubernetes `agent-sandbox` clusters or attach external multi-cluster cloud pools via the standard E2B protocol (`e2bpool`).
    - **Runtime Images**: Maintain versioned base images and upgrade plans across SkyBot Dev Machines, Tasks, and Automations.
    - **Runtime Monitor**: Inspect live sandbox instances and health across the deployment (`runtimes?view=overview`).
  </Tab>
  <Tab value="Models & Memory Engine">
    Under **Model Services**, operators configure:
    - **AI Gateway**: Manage deployment-wide LLM and Embedding gateway endpoints and platform credentials.
    - **Platform Assistant**: Configure the backend model and capability scope powering the global helper.
    - **Memory & Agent CLI**: Administer PostgreSQL-backed Mem0 memory distillation settings and curated Agent CLI catalogs.
  </Tab>
  <Tab value="Enterprise IM & Connectors">
    Under **Organization Integrations** and **Connector Providers**, Org Admins manage SSO and external doc providers:
    - **Lark / Feishu & Slack Integration**: Configure region, App ID, write-only App Secret, and `Public Base URL`—changes take effect immediately without restarting. Register a single OAuth callback URL per provider (`/auth/enterprise/lark/callback`, `/auth/enterprise/feishu/callback`, or `/auth/enterprise/slack/callback`).
    - **Auto-Provisioning Policy**: When enabled, new logins auto-create a `Member` solely in `Public Workspace`. When disabled, an explicit `IdentityLink` must exist beforehand—accounts never auto-merge by email.
    - **Official Lark/Feishu CLI Grants**: Member OAuth requests user-scoped permissions defined by the enterprise app; SkyMythos records only the actual scopes returned by the provider.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
