## Eight-Role Enterprise Adoption Matrix [#eight-role-adoption-matrix]

Aligned with our [Role Solutions](/solutions), teams across eight core business functions typically start with these combinations:

| Core Function | High-Frequency Use Cases | Recommended Surfaces | Required Knowledge & Tools | Key Human Checkpoints |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **HR** | Policy Q&A, job description drafting, candidate evaluation summaries | SkyBot Projects + Knowledge + AI Employees | Published Employee Handbook and hiring rubrics | Hiring decisions, compensation offers, external emails |
| **Finance** | Expense policy pre-checks, variance commentary drafts, clause lookup | AI Employees + Knowledge + Automations | Published Travel & Expense Policy; sanitized read-only sheets | Payment approvals, official financial reporting, ledger posts |
| **Legal** | Contract clause comparison checklists, baseline reviews, risk briefs | SkyBot Projects + isolated Knowledge Base | Dedicated restricted Legal Knowledge Base and templates | Final legal opinions, contract signatures, risk sign-offs |
| **Sales** | Account discovery briefs, proposal assembly from specs, call summaries | SkyBot + Knowledge + Lark/Feishu Connector | Sanitized whitepapers, FAQs, and approved case studies | Commercial pricing commitments, discounts, external sends |
| **Marketing & Ops** | Feedback attribution, campaign retrospectives, content calendar drafts | SkyBot Projects + AI Employees + Automations | Brand guidelines, sanitized campaign metrics, style manuals | Public publishing, ad spend changes, official announcements |
| **Product** | User interview synthesis, structured PRD drafts, acceptance checklists | SkyBot Projects + Knowledge + Tasks (read) | Historical product decisions, architecture docs, design specs | Roadmap prioritization, release scope sign-off |
| **Engineering** | Feature delivery, test coverage, code review pre-screening, triage | Personal Dev Machine + Tasks + Flows | Personal Git credential, coding convention Skills, Unit Gates | Architecture sign-off, Git merges, production deployments |
| **IT & Management** | Egress audits, ticket triage, team usage and cost governance | Observability + Team/Platform Admin + Connectors | Model gateway catalogs, sandbox pools, SSO (`IdentityLink`) | Role grants/revocations, production changes, gateway approvals |
