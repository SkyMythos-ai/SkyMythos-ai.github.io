The real payoff of an enterprise AI Harness is not a single lucky run—it is **making sure the next task starts stronger without repeating past trial and error**. This case shows how to evolve completed work into reusable AI Employees and Skills.

## Goal & Prerequisites [#goal-and-prerequisites]

- **Expected Deliverable**: A configured "API Convention Reviewer" AI Employee paired with a reusable workspace `Skill` for teammates.
- **Prerequisites**: At least one completed SkyBot or engineering run with verified results, plus permission to author AI Employees and Marketplace assets.

## Step-by-Step Walkthrough [#step-by-step-walkthrough]

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### 1. Draft an AI Employee from Experience

    Open **AI Employees** (`my-ai?section=employees`), click **Create Employee**, and switch to **Learn from Work Experience**. State the target role (e.g., "Pre-screen new REST API changes against team error-code and pagination standards").
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### 2. Select Valid Sources & Review the Proposal

    Pick eligible work experience records (avoiding entries flagged ineligible or pending), click **Analyze Selected Experience**, inspect the proposed name, prompt, and sources, and click **Use Proposal & Edit Employee**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### 3. Complete Model & Scope Settings

    Verify the model, workspace visibility, and minimal required Skills or read-only Knowledge Bases, then click **Create Employee** to save immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### 4. Refine Over Time via Explicit Training Confirmation

    As the employee handles new tasks, open its detail view and run **Train Employee Definition**. Review the generated revision diff carefully and click **Confirm Training & Save Results** (remember: this updates instructions and bindings—not underlying LLM weights—and leaves in-flight snapshots untouched).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Acceptance Checklist [#acceptance-checklist]

- **Validate Before Sharing**: Only promote workflows you have personally verified on real work.
- **Re-Test After Training**: Run a comparable small task after each training update to verify consistent output.
