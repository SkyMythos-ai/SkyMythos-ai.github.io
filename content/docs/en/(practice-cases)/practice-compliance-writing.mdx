title: "Practice 2: Policy Retrieval & Compliance Drafting"
description: Combine permission-isolated Knowledge Bases with specialized AI Employees to draft HR and legal compliance memos under strict human sign-off.
---

When HR, Legal, or Finance teams draft policy notices, role adjustment agreements, or contract risk checklists, every clause must align strictly with active company policies while keeping sensitive documents restricted to authorized departments.

## Goal & Prerequisites [#goal-and-prerequisites]

- **Expected Deliverable**: A draft "Role Adjustment Agreement" paired with a "Cited Policy Cross-Reference Table" and a "Pre-Signing Verification Checklist."
- **Prerequisites**:
  1. Publish the active "Employee Handbook" and "Standard Employment Contract Templates" inside an access-restricted departmental Workspace Knowledge Base.
  2. Configure an AI Employee (e.g., "HR Employee Relations Specialist") scoped strictly to that Knowledge Base.

## Step-by-Step Walkthrough [#step-by-step-walkthrough]

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### 1. Start Collaboration with the Specialist

    Open [AI Employees](/docs/employees) and click **Start Collaboration** on "HR Employee Relations Specialist" inside your authorized workspace, then upload sanitized discussion notes.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### 2. Submit the Structured Drafting Request

    ```text
    Goal: Draft a "Mutual Role Adjustment Confirmation" combining our Employee Handbook transfer rules with the attached notes.
    Materials: Query only the published Employee Handbook and Standard Contract Templates in this workspace.
    Deliverables: Output the draft agreement, a clause-by-clause citation table, and a checklist of supporting records and sign-off dates HR must verify.
    Boundaries: Draft for HR and Legal review only; never finalize seals or send directly to employees.
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### 3. Audit Clause Citations & Finalize Manually

    Verify every cited handbook chapter against the live published revision, then route the draft through HR and Legal leads for final approval and execution.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Acceptance Checklist [#acceptance-checklist]

- **Verify Access Isolation**: Confirm departmental policies remain scoped to the Workspace and were not accidentally published as Organization-public releases.
- **Human Legal & HR Sign-Off**: All formal notices, compensation adjustments, and contract executions stay under human accountability.
